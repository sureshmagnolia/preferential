Institutional Single Transferable Vote (STV) Framework

The Single Transferable Vote (STV) is a proportional preferential electoral system utilized internationally for university senates, municipal councils, and national parliaments. It allows electors to express preferences freely without risk of vote splitting or strategic voting.

1. The Droop Quota

Calculates the exact quota necessary and sufficient to elect the required vacancies:

$$\text{Quota} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Valid Votes}}{\text{Vacancies} + 1} \right\rfloor + 1$$

2. Fractional Transfer Value

Surplus votes are transferred down to subsequent candidate preferences at a fractional value:

$$\text{Transfer Factor} = \frac{\text{Votes Received} - \text{Quota}}{\text{Votes Received}}$$

3. Lowest Candidate Elimination

If no hopeful candidate reaches the quota, the lowest-polling candidate is eliminated. Their ballots transfer at 100% of their current value to next active preferences.

Audit Trail & Verification Integrity

Every ballot logged into this system is permanently assigned a distinct Serial Number (e.g. BAL-0042). Scrutineers, candidates, and returning officers can inspect any individual ballot slip in the registry, verify the physical paper preferences entered, and audit how those votes transferred during the count.
